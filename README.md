# 🏠 LetRent

### Rent Management. Made Easy.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-letrent.in-blue?style=flat&logo=google-chrome)](https://letrent.in)

**LetRent** is a rental management platform designed to simplify the renting experience for tenants, property owners, and property managers.

Our mission is to make rental management easier, more transparent, and more convenient through technology.

## 🚀 What We're Building

LetRent brings essential rental management operations together in one platform.

- **Property Management** – Manage multiple rental properties from a single platform.
- **Rent Payments** – Send and receive rent payments through multiple payment methods.
- **Payment Tracking** – Keep track of rent payments, pending dues, and transactions.
- **Maintenance Requests** – Create and track property maintenance requests.
- **Smart Dashboards** – Get a clear overview of rental activities and property information.
- **Multiple Profiles** – Dedicated experiences for tenants, property owners, and managers.

## 🎯 Our Vision

We believe managing rental properties should be simple.

We're building technology that reduces the complexity of rental management, improves communication between stakeholders, and makes rent payments seamless.

## 🛠️ Technology

We use modern technologies and engineering practices to build reliable, scalable, and user-friendly products.
